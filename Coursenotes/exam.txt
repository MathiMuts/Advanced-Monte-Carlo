## Assignment
You are required to solve problems 4.1–4.8 and 4.12–4.16 from the lecture notes. In addition, you should solve at least one of problems 4.9, 4.10, and 4.11.
You are welcome to go beyond these requirements and explore additional problems or systems if you wish.
Once you have completed the assignment, prepare a short report presenting your solutions and the results of your simulations. There is no strict page limit, but approximately 25 pages should be sufficient. Please compile everything into a single PDF file and upload it to Toledo at least one day before your exam.
Your report should include graphs showing the results of your simulations, accompanied by brief explanatory text. Make sure that all plotted quantities are clearly identified, that the axes are properly labelled, and that you describe the relevant simulation details, including the parameters used, the number of samples, and any other information necessary to understand and interpret your results.

## Exam format
The exam consists of a written part followed by an oral part, both taking place on the same day.
During the written part, you will answer a few questions based on the material covered in the lecture notes. You will work on paper, without access to books, lecture notes, or any other materials.
For the oral part, which will follow you should bring your written solutions. We will discuss these solutions together with your simulation report for approximately 15–20 minutes. We will go through the assignment, and I will ask you questions about your solutions, your simulations, and the underlying theoretical concepts.

I hope these instructions are clear. If you have any questions, please do not hesitate to contact me.
